If you've worked any job, then you're most likely familiar with the concept of a probation period. This is where a company hires you, but you're on a 90-day trial to see whether you're a good fit for the company. During this trial your contract can be terminated for pretty much any reason the employer deems fit. When Jesus freely gives us eternal life, does the everlasting, all-knowing God treat us the same way? Does He give us a timeframe to prove ourselves, otherwise we're kicked out of His family? Most people would say, "No, God does not kick us out, but we can forfeit it" or "No, but we can reject the gift".&#x20;
